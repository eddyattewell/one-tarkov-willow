**Why this build is the quiet one**

Nothing an information overlay draws is unusual on its own. Looking at the correct
angle, holding a pre-aim on a corner, checking a room before entering — these are
things a good player does constantly. The overlay just removes the guessing. It is
substantially harder to notice than aim assistance, which is why it is the module
most long-term users settle on.

**Configuration that stays readable**

| **Situation** | **Suggestion** |
| --- | --- |
| Close-quarters map | boxes + health, skeleton off |
| Open map | radar + range, boxes thin |
| Crowded team fight | boxes only |
| Looting phase | items + radar |

**Clutter is the real enemy**

Every layer you enable competes for attention. A screen with six overlays active
is harder to read than one with two, and the information advantage disappears into
noise. Pick two layers and keep them.

**Order of operations**

1. Close the game and its launcher completely.
2. Run the loader as administrator.
3. Open the **Spoofer** tab and press **Spoof**.
4. **Reboot** — volume serial and SMBIOS are read at boot.
5. Create a fresh account and log in.

**What it does not do**

It does not unban the old account. It gives the machine a new identity so a new
account is not linked to the old ban.

**Skipping the reboot is the most common mistake** — people report "the spoofer
did nothing" when they simply never restarted.
